中文

数据保存在哪里？

病历、药品、附件和提醒记录默认保存在本机，并使用加密存储。应用不提供账号或云同步。

如何备份或换机？

进入“设置”，选择“导出加密备份”，设置备份密码并选择保存位置。在新设备中安装应用后，选择“导入加密备份”。密码不会上传或保留。

识别失败怎么办？

请保持光线充足并让包装或报告位于取景框内。识别失败时可以直接手工录入，所有结果在保存前都需要确认。

重要说明

本应用只提供记录和提醒，不提供诊断、处方或治疗建议。出现紧急健康问题，请联系医疗机构。

English

Where is my data stored?

Medical records, medicines, attachments, and reminders are stored on this device using encrypted storage. There is no account or cloud sync.

How do I back up or move devices?

Open Settings, choose Export encrypted backup, set a password, and select a destination. On the new device, install the app and choose Import encrypted backup. The password is not uploaded or retained.

What if recognition fails?

Use good lighting and keep the package or report inside the frame. You can enter values manually, and all recognition results require confirmation before saving.

Important notice

This app records and reminds. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or recommend treatment. Contact a medical professional for urgent health concerns.
