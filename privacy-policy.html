中文

应用定位

“该吃药了”是一款本地优先的家庭病历、药品和提醒记录工具。应用只用于保存和整理用户主动录入的信息，不提供诊断、处方、药物相互作用判断或治疗建议。

数据保存位置

应用不需要账号，不提供云同步。档案名称、病历、医院和医生信息、药品信息、附件、提醒和服药记录默认只保存在你的设备上。本地数据库和导入的附件会加密保存。我们不会把健康数据上传到服务器。

不会收集或共享的数据

我们不收集、出售或共享健康数据、联系人、位置、广告标识符或其他个人资料。应用不包含广告 SDK、分析 SDK 或远程崩溃上报。Android 正式版本不申请联网权限。

设备权限

相机用于拍摄药品包装或报告；照片和文件权限用于导入你选择的附件；通知和精确闹钟用于发送提醒；生物识别通过系统 API 解锁应用，应用不会读取、保存或上传指纹或面容模板。

端侧识别

文字识别和条码识别在本机完成，图片和识别结果不会上传。识别结果必须经过你确认后才会保存。

加密导出和删除

你可以主动导出密码保护的 .fha 备份。备份文件和密码由你控制。你可以在应用内删除数据，也可以清除应用存储或卸载应用；已导出的文件需要由你自行删除。

儿童和家人资料

本应用面向成年人使用。录入家人或未满 14 周岁儿童资料前，请确认你已取得本人或法定监护人的授权。

联系我们

如有隐私或支持问题，请联系 takeyourpills.app@outlook.com。

English

Purpose

Take your pills is a local-first organizer for family medical records, medicines, and reminders. It records information entered by the user and does not diagnose, prescribe, evaluate drug interactions, or recommend treatment.

Data storage

The app does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization. Profiles, medical records, hospital and doctor details, medicine details, attachments, reminders, and medication history are stored on the device by default. The local database and imported attachments are encrypted on the device. We do not upload health data to our servers.

Collection and sharing

We do not collect, sell, or share health data, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or other personal profile information. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or remote crash reporting. The Android release does not request network permission.

Permissions and on-device recognition

Camera, photo, and file access support user-selected imports. Notifications and exact alarms deliver reminders. Biometrics use the platform authentication interface and never expose templates to the app. OCR and barcode recognition run on the device and results are saved only after confirmation.

Export and deletion

You may export a password-protected .fha backup. You control the file and password. You can delete data in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files must be deleted separately.

Children and family members

The app is intended for adults. Before entering information for a family member or a child under 14, confirm that you have authorization from that person or their legal guardian.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact takeyourpills.app@outlook.com.
